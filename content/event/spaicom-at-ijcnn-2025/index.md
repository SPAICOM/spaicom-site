IJCNN 2025, the International Joint Conference on Neural Networks, was held from 30 June to 5 July 2025 in Rome, at the Pontifical Gregorian University.

Mario Edoardo Pandolfo and Simone Fiorellino presented **Latent Space Alignment for AI-Native MIMO Semantic Communications**, based on the paper [Latent Space Alignment for AI-Native MIMO Semantic Communications]({{< relref "publication/latent-space-alignment-for-ainative-mimo-semantic-communications" >}}), co-authored with Emilio Calvanese Strinati and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper introduces a MIMO precoder/decoder pair that jointly performs latent space compression and semantic channel equalization, addressing both semantic mismatches and physical channel impairments.
