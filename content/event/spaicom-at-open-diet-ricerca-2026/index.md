The Department of Information Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunications (DIET) of Sapienza University of Rome invites everyone to **Open DIET Ricerca 2026**.

The day presents and promotes the research activities of the Department, creating opportunities for exchange between the DIET scientific community, students, and external partners from academia and industry. Through talks and poster sessions designed for a broad and diverse audience, the initiative aims to share results and expertise, foster new collaborations, and bring students closer to the master's and doctoral programs connected to the Department.

The event is aimed at students, faculty, and researchers of the two Faculties of Engineering and of other Sapienza Departments. It is also open to faculty and researchers from the other universities in Rome, as well as to companies and startups in the area. The talks offer an accessible overview of the Department's activities, of the collaborations developed, and of possible future directions.

## SPAICOM at Open DIET Ricerca

**When and where:** Thursday 26 November 2026, from 10:00, in the Sala and Courtyard of the Chiostro of the Faculty of Engineering, San Pietro in Vincoli, Via Eudossiana 18, Rome. More information on the [official event page](https://diet.web.uniroma1.it/it/open-diet-ricerca-2026).

The SPAICOM group will present three posters:

1. **Sheaf-theoretic Signal Processing**, based on the paper [Sheaf-theoretic Signal Processing on Graphs: Spectral Theory, Filtering, and Sampling]({{< relref "publication/sheaftheoretic-signal-processing-on-graphs-spectral-theory-filtering-and-sampling" >}}). Presented by Gabriele D'Acunto.
2. **A Sheaf-Theoretic Framework for Distributed Multi-Site Channel Charting**, based on the paper [A Sheaf-Theoretic Framework for Distributed Multi-Site Channel Charting]({{< relref "publication/a-sheaftheoretic-framework-for-distributed-multisite-channel-charting" >}}). Presented by Enrico Grimaldi.
3. **Over-the-air Semantic Alignment with Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces**, based on the paper [Over-the-Air Semantic Alignment with Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces]({{< relref "publication/overtheair-semantic-alignment-with-stacked-intelligent-metasurfaces" >}}). Presented by Mario Edoardo Pandolfo.

Come and meet the group at our posters.
