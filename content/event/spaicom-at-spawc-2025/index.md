SPAWC 2025, the IEEE 26th International Workshop on Signal Processing and Artificial Intelligence for Wireless Communications, was held in Surrey, United Kingdom, from 7 to 10 July 2025.

Giuseppe Di Poce presented [Federated Latent Space Alignment for Multi-User Semantic Communications]({{< relref "publication/federated-latent-space-alignment-for-multiuser-semantic-communications" >}}), co-authored with Mario Edoardo Pandolfo, Emilio Calvanese Strinati, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper proposes a federated protocol for aligning semantic representations between an access point and multiple users under power and complexity constraints.
