The ELLIS Unit Madrid Graph Signal Processing Workshop 2026 was held in Madrid from 8 to 10 June 2026.

SPAICOM gave a poster, two presentations, and a tutorial:

- Enrico Grimaldi and Tiziana Cattai presented a poster.
- Gabriele D'Acunto gave a presentation.
- Leonardo Di Nino gave a presentation on [Learning Dirac Spectral Transforms for Topological Signals]({{< relref "publication/learning-dirac-spectral-transforms-for-topological-signals" >}}), co-authored with Tiziana Cattai, Sergio Barbarossa, Ginestra Bianconi, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper learns a spectral transform that captures cross-domain interactions between node and edge signals on topological spaces.
- Paolo Di Lorenzo gave a tutorial.
