SPAWC 2026, the IEEE 27th International Workshop on Signal Processing and Artificial Intelligence for Wireless Communications, was held in Athens, Greece, from 6 to 9 September 2026.

Enrico Grimaldi presented [A Sheaf-Theoretic Framework for Distributed Multi-Site Channel Charting]({{< relref "publication/a-sheaftheoretic-framework-for-distributed-multisite-channel-charting" >}}), co-authored with Leonardo Di Nino, Mario Edoardo Pandolfo, Gabriele D'Acunto, Sergio Barbarossa, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper models multi-site channel charting as a network sheaf to align locally learned representations across base stations.
