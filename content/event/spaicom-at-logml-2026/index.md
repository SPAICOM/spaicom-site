LOGML 2026, the London Geometry and Machine Learning Summer School, was held in London from 13 to 17 July 2026.

Leonardo Di Nino, Lorenzo Marinucci, and Mario Edoardo Pandolfo attended on behalf of SPAICOM, where two posters were presented:

- Leonardo Di Nino presented [Structured Sheaf Learning of Consistent Connection Graphs]({{< relref "publication/structured-sheaf-learning-of-consistent-connection-graphs" >}}), co-authored with Gabriele D'Acunto, Sergio Barbarossa, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper jointly learns a connection graph's topology and geometric structure from noisy signals.
- Mario Edoardo Pandolfo presented [SEMASIA: A Large-Scale Dataset of Semantically Structured Latent Representations]({{< relref "publication/semasia-a-largescale-dataset-of-semantically-structured-latent-representations" >}}), co-authored with Enrico Grimaldi, Lorenzo Marinucci, Leonardo Di Nino, Simone Fiorellino, Sergio Barbarossa, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper introduces a large-scale dataset of latent representations from pretrained vision models for studying semantic alignment.
