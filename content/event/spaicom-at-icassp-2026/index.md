ICASSP 2026, the IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing, was held from 4 to 8 May 2026 at the Centre de Convencions Internacional de Barcelona (CCIB).

SPAICOM presented three papers:

1. [Learning the Structure of Connection Graphs]({{< relref "publication/learning-the-structure-of-connection-graphs" >}}), by Leonardo Di Nino, Gabriele D'Acunto, Sergio Barbarossa, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper learns a connection graph's topology and geometric structure directly from observed signals.
2. [Learning Consistent Causal Abstraction Networks]({{< relref "publication/learning-consistent-causal-abstraction-networks" >}}), by Gabriele D'Acunto, Paolo Di Lorenzo, and Sergio Barbarossa. The paper proposes a sheaf-theoretic framework for learning consistent causal abstractions between structural causal models.
3. [Simplicial Gaussian Models: Representation and Inference]({{< relref "publication/simplicial-gaussian-models-representation-and-inference" >}}), by Lorenzo Marinucci, Gabriele D'Acunto, Paolo Di Lorenzo, and Sergio Barbarossa. The paper extends Gaussian graphical models to simplicial complexes, jointly modeling variables on vertices, edges, and triangles.
