The 2025 59th Asilomar Conference on Signals, Systems, and Computers was held from 26 to 29 October 2025 at the Asilomar Hotel and Conference Grounds in Pacific Grove, California.

Enrico Grimaldi and Mario Edoardo Pandolfo attended on behalf of SPAICOM, where two papers were presented:

1. Enrico Grimaldi presented [Learning Network Sheaves for AI-native Semantic Communication]({{< relref "publication/learning-network-sheaves-for-ainative-semantic-communication" >}}), co-authored with Mario Edoardo Pandolfo, Gabriele D'Acunto, Sergio Barbarossa, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper learns how AI agents should align their internal representations to communicate efficiently.
2. Mario Edoardo Pandolfo presented [Colored Markov Random Fields for Probabilistic Topological Modeling]({{< relref "publication/colored-markov-random-fields-for-probabilistic-topological-modeling" >}}), co-authored with Lorenzo Marinucci, Leonardo Di Nino, Gabriele D'Acunto, Paolo Di Lorenzo, and Sergio Barbarossa. The paper introduces a graphical model for data defined on topological spaces.
