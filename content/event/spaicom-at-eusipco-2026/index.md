EUSIPCO 2026, the European Signal Processing Conference, was held in Bruges, Belgium, from 31 August to 4 September 2026.

Paolo Di Lorenzo gave a tutorial at the conference.

Leonardo Di Nino and Mario Edoardo Pandolfo each presented a poster:

- Leonardo Di Nino presented [Learning Dirac Spectral Transforms for Topological Signals]({{< relref "publication/learning-dirac-spectral-transforms-for-topological-signals" >}}), co-authored with Tiziana Cattai, Sergio Barbarossa, Ginestra Bianconi, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper learns a spectral transform that captures cross-domain interactions between node and edge signals on topological spaces.
- Mario Edoardo Pandolfo presented [Over-the-Air Semantic Alignment with Stacked Intelligent Metasurfaces]({{< relref "publication/overtheair-semantic-alignment-with-stacked-intelligent-metasurfaces" >}}), co-authored with Kyriakos Stylianopoulos, George C. Alexandropoulos, and Paolo Di Lorenzo. The paper performs latent-space alignment directly over the wireless channel using programmable metasurfaces.
